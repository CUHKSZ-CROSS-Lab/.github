## 🥇Vision & Mission

> **"Leveraging CUHK(Shenzhen)'s strengths in artificial intelligence and interdisciplinary research, high-level talent cultivation and research organization, together with the practical foundation of Shenzhen Kuafu-Yundun Technology Co., Ltd. in full-chain cross-border e-commerce operations, industrial collaboration networks, digital data resources platforms and industrialization, the Joint Laboratory conducts collaborative research and application validation around 'AI + Cross-border E-commerce + Industrial Digitalization'."**

The Joint Laboratory is committed to building a **joint innovation system for cross-border e-commerce operational decision-making, supply-chain collaboration, risk control and manufacturing empowerment**, continuously strengthening three core capabilities:

| 📚️ Research Impact | 🏭 Industrial Service | 🎓 Talent Cultivation |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Frontier algorithms & theory<br>Top-tier papers · patents · standards | Real-world validation & iteration<br>Reusable data & algorithm capabilities | Co-supervision system<br>MPhil / PhD / Postdoctoral |

<a id="research"></a>
## 🔬 Research Directions

### 1️⃣ Data Governance & Data Assetization for Cross-border E-commerce
> Building a **high-quality cross-border e-commerce data foundation** for both research and industrial application.

Multi-source heterogeneous data spanning marketplaces, enterprise ERP, procurement, inventory, logistics, finance, supply chain and external industry sources; research on cross-platform data standards, master-data systems, metric definitions, data-quality evaluation, data assetization, and security & compliance methodologies.

`Data Standards` `Master Data` `Metric Definitions` `Data Quality` `Data Assetization` `Compliance`

### 2️⃣ Intelligent Operational Decision-making & Forecasting
> Delivering **verifiable, interpretable and iterable** decision models and evaluation systems.

Sales & market demand forecasting, inventory & replenishment decisions, profit & operational efficiency analytics, category & product opportunity identification — powered by time-series analysis, machine learning, operations research and AI.

`Demand Forecasting` `Replenishment` `Business Analytics` `Opportunity Mining` `Time Series` `Optimization`

### 3️⃣ Risk Identification & Intelligent Risk Control
> Forging **risk monitoring and decision-support capabilities** for enterprises and industrial collaboration.

Operational, inventory, capital, supply-chain, compliance and market-volatility risks; multi-dimensional risk indicator systems, early-warning rules, risk scoring and intelligent analysis methods.

`Risk Indicators` `Early Warning` `Risk Scoring` `Compliance Control` `Supply-chain Risk`

### 4️⃣ Reverse Cloud Product Selection & Manufacturing Collaboration
> **Reverse-empowering** manufacturers' R&D, production and supply chains with market-side data.

Spanning the full chain of `market demand → product design → procurement → factory production → cross-border fulfillment → end-sale`, researching demand insight, competitive analysis, product opportunity assessment, capacity matching, and production & stocking planning.

`Reverse Selection` `Demand Insight` `Competitive Analysis` `Capacity Matching` `Production Planning` `Manufacturing Synergy`

### 5️⃣ AI Applications & Technology Transfer
> Exploring the **applicability boundaries and engineering blueprints** of large models in cross-border e-commerce.

LLMs, agents, knowledge bases and intelligent analytics for cross-border e-commerce BI, risk control, industry research, smart reporting and business collaboration; mature outcomes are validated and transferred in real industrial scenarios.

`LLM` `Agents` `Knowledge Base` `Intelligent BI` `Smart Reporting` `Tech Transfer`
